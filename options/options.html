Privacy and security

External translation sends selected readable text—or writing explicitly submitted through Smart Compose—over HTTPS. The Privacy Firewall masks common identifiable values first. The developer receives no text. Login, password, passcode, PIN, one-time-code, security-code and payment fields, cookies, full HTML, images and files are never included. No analytics, ads or telemetry are present.
