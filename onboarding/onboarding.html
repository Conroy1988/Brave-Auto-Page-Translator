INDEPENDENT EXTENSION

Translation on your terms.

Choose what the extension may access and where webpage text is processed before anything is translated.

01

Your language

Foreign-language pages will be translated into this language.

02

When translation may run

Website access is requested only for the mode you choose.

03

Translation provider

The automatic option prefers the browser's on-device engine when available, then uses configured providers and finally the disclosed Google web compatibility service.

Privacy checkpoint

When an external provider is used, readable webpage text is sent over HTTPS to that provider for translation. Text may contain personal or sensitive information. The extension does not intentionally send page URLs, cookies, passwords, form entries, full HTML, images or files. The developer does not receive page text and the extension contains no analytics or advertising.
